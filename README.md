# Parking Brake Assembly

## Project Overview

  This repository contains the CAD models, technical drawings, and design documentation for the **cable-operated parking-brake assembly** 
The project focuses on engineering a reliable, lightweight, and scrutineering-compliant parking brake system for the team's solar vehicle, integrating custom mechanical components with robust chassis mounting solutions.


## Key Features & Design Components
**Hand-Cranked Winch System:**
  
* Designed a custom winding mechanism and pulley setup inspired by high-tension cable systems (such as volleyball net cranks).
* Integrated a secure locking mechanism to maintain continuous cable tension and prevent accidental unwinding.
* Transitioned toward commercial cable winch solutions featuring spring-loaded pawls and gear locking to optimize weight, safety, and reliability.


**Anti-Slip Two-Piece Chassis Clamp:**
* Engineered a robust split-clamp architecture to mount securely onto the vehicle's chassis tubes.
* Designed to prevent sliding or rotation along the frame under high tension, providing a stable platform for components.


**Optimized Pulley & Cable Routing:**
* Conducted motion and interference studies in SolidWorks to map out smooth cable routing from the interior lever location to the rear bike brake assembly.
* Utilized grooved sheaves/pulleys to eliminate high-friction contact points with the chassis and evenly distribute bending loads across the cable.

## Engineering Challenge & Resolution

  During the design review phase, we encountered a critical setback regarding the custom hand-cranked winch mechanism. Following design feedback, it became evident that our initial custom-machined winch concept suffered from an unpredictable locking pawl mechanism, high manufacturing costs, and excessive weight due to the thick steel required for structural integrity. Additionally, custom machining or 3D printing the intricate gear teeth posed significant risks to mechanical precision and load-bearing strength.

  To resolve this technically without compromising our timeline, we pivoted from a fully custom build to integrating a commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) cable winch featuring a pre-engineered spring-loaded ratchet pawl and a larger drum diameter for smoother, continuous tensioning. We then focused our custom CAD efforts on designing a specialized two-piece split chassis clamp to securely interface the commercial winch with the solar car's frame at an ergonomic angle for driver accessibility. This experience reinforced the importance of balancing custom engineering with Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles—recognizing when to leverage reliable, cost-effective commercial hardware so engineering bandwidth can be concentrated on custom integration and system packaging.


## Engineering Process & Considerations
* **Design for Manufacturing (DFM) & Assembly (DFA):** Refined designs through iterative CAD studies to balance structural rigidity with weight reduction. Evaluated trade-offs between custom machining/3D printing versus commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) components (e.g., standard sheaves and winches) to maximize precision and cost-effectiveness.


* **Scrutineering Compliance:** Optimized passing margins and mechanical reliability to ensure the parking brake meets safety regulations.
